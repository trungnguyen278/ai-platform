## 2026-10-28 — Two channels

**Done:** Hosting the cloud for nothing showed a flaw in the cloud link. A device stays in its LiveKit room all day, so one robot and its agent spend about 86,400 participant-minutes a month: LiveKit Cloud's free plan has 5,000. An idle agent also waits for each device, and the media server must take UDP all the time. Alexa and xiaozhi keep a light channel open, and stream audio only to talk.

**Decisions:** [ADR-0006](../decisions/0006-two-channels.md), proposed: a control channel always on (a WebSocket over HTTPS), and the room only for conversations. This is the cloud link 3.0, with the renames to devices and homes. A wake word opens a conversation of many turns, as the owner wants, and PRIV-1 now speaks of conversations.

**Next:** The cloud link 3.0's specification.

## 2026-10-28 — The cloud link 3.0

**Done:** [The cloud link 3.0](../../protocols/cloud/README.md), its schema, and four examples: a session, a conversation, the cloud's reminders, and a resumed session.
- The session runs on a WebSocket at `/device/v3/link`, opened with the device's access token. The major version is in the path, so a retired one gets `410 Gone`: update required.
- Messages are numbered in each session, and pings say what was received. A session then survives a short loss of its connection and resends what was missed, as Discord's gateway does. Cloudflare closes quiet WebSockets, and ends them when it releases code. Before it closes a connection on purpose, the cloud sends `reconnect`, as Gemini Live sends `GoAway`.
- A wake word asks for a conversation, and the cloud answers with a LiveKit room and its token. What the device heard before the agent joined goes as LiveKit's pre-connect audio. The agent takes the turns, and says so in the room, with the audio. Calls to the device, the agent's included, go on the control channel. The device ends a conversation on its own after `idle` or `longest` (PRIV-1).
- A conversation the cloud opens listens only if the owner allows the `privacy` class. Otherwise it only speaks.
- Devices: the `hello` gives the kind, the model, the languages, the time zone and the allowed classes. ADR-0004's conventions apply: UUIDs, RFC 3339, durations in seconds.
- The requirements now speak of every device. IO-2 and SEC-8 speak of conversations.

**Decisions:** [ADR-0006](../decisions/0006-two-channels.md) revised, still proposed. The owner asked whether two choices of the first draft would change the link's structure:
- a conversation's turns and answers now stay in its room, since the agent makes and reads them. Every call to a device still goes on the control channel;
- the pings stay the protocol's own. Alexa's SDK, Discord and LiveKit do the same on links that stay open for hours, while WebSocket's Ping frames stop at a proxy that ends the WebSocket. "To run over MQTT later" was a wrong reason: MQTT has its own keep-alive.
